# CREDIT MOOC

**Green & Circular Economy Learning Platform**

CREDIT MOOC is the free online course of the CREDIT project — *Green & Circular Economy in Business by Digital Solutions*. In six modules and thirty short units, it helps entrepreneurs and employees of small and micro-enterprises put green and circular-economy practices to work in their own organisations. Learners who complete the course receive a verifiable certificate of completion.

- Platform: https://etizebrar.github.io/green-mooc-platform/
- Project: https://erasmuscredit.eu
